Glossary

AETR
An international agreement on drivers' hours for journeys to or through some countries outside the EU.
Annual test
The yearly roadworthiness test for lorries over 3,500kg and many trailers (the HGV MOT).
Assimilated rules
The drivers' hours rules kept in UK law from the former EU rules. They apply to most goods vehicles over 3.5 tonnes.
Design gross weight (DGW)
The most a vehicle is designed to weigh when loaded. It decides a van's MOT class.
DVLA
Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency: driving licences, vehicle registration and tax.
DVSA
Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency: testing, roadside enforcement and roadworthiness guidance.
Earned Recognition
A DVSA scheme for operators who prove high standards through audited systems. Members' vehicles are less likely to be stopped.
EBPMS
Electronic brake performance monitoring system: a way to assess trailer brakes using data from normal use.
First use inspection
An inspection before a newly acquired, hired or borrowed vehicle goes on the road.
GB domestic rules
The drivers' hours rules for goods vehicles outside the assimilated rules, such as most vans used only in Great Britain.
Gross train weight (GTW)
The most a vehicle and its trailer can weigh together when loaded.
Inspection notice
An advisory notice from a DVSA examiner for defects not serious enough for a prohibition.
LOLER
Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998: thorough examinations for tail lifts, cranes and other lifting equipment.
Maximum authorised mass (MAM)
The most a vehicle can weigh when fully loaded. Licence categories are based on it.
Nil defect report
A driver's report confirming the walkaround check was done and no defects were found.
O-licence
A goods vehicle operator's licence, needed for most goods vehicles over 3,500kg.
OCRS
Operator Compliance Risk Score: DVSA's risk rating for operators, based on annual test and roadside check results.
PG10
The form that removes a prohibition once the vehicle has been re-examined and found fit.
PG9
A prohibition notice: a ban on using a vehicle on the road, either immediately or after a delay.
Plated weight
The legal maximum weights shown on a goods vehicle's plate.
Roller brake test (RBT)
A brake test on rollers that measures each wheel's braking. Most effective when the vehicle is laden.
S-marking
A note on a prohibition showing a significant failure of roadworthiness compliance.
Safety inspection
A planned inspection at the interval recorded on VOL. Often called a PMI (periodic maintenance inspection).
Safety-critical
A defect or system that could affect the control or directional stability of a vehicle.
Smart 2 tachograph
The latest tachograph generation, required in newly registered goods vehicles that need a tachograph.
Traffic Commissioners
The regulators who grant operator licences and can act against them.
Undertakings
The promises an operator makes to get a licence, such as inspecting vehicles at set intervals and keeping records.
VOL
Vehicle Operator Licensing: the online system that holds operator licence details, including inspection intervals.
VOR
Vehicle off road: a vehicle that can't be used until it's repaired.
Walkaround check
The daily check by the driver or another responsible person before the vehicle is used.
Zero-emission vehicle
A vehicle without an internal combustion engine, or with one that emits zero grams of CO2 per km.
