Recent rule changes

All law updates →
  • 1 July 2026

    Vans over 2.5 tonnes on international journeys

    Assimilated drivers' hours rules now apply, and smart 2 tachographs are needed for international hire or reward work.

  • 1 June 2026

    Electric vans up to 4.25 tonnes

    Zero-emission vans of 3,501kg to 4,250kg moved to the Class 7 MOT, and are exempt from drivers' hours and tachograph rules in Great Britain.

  • 24 December 2025

    Smart 2 tachographs for new goods vehicles

    Newly registered goods vehicles that need a tachograph must have a full smart 2 tachograph.
