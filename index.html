Recent changes

All law updates →
  • 14 October 2026

    LED headlamp bulbs can pass the MOT

    Approved LED replacement bulbs in halogen headlamps can now pass class 4, 5 and 7 MOTs, if the approval sticker is fixed where the tester can see it.

  • 1 June 2026

    Electric vans up to 4.25 tonnes

    Zero-emission vans of 3,501kg to 4,250kg moved from the heavy vehicle annual test to the Class 7 MOT, and follow the 1.6mm tread rule.

  • 2 February 2026

    Prohibition clearances by email

    DVSA now emails PG10 clearance notices to the operator's email address linked to the vehicle on VOL.
