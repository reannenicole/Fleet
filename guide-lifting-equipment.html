Lifting equipment and plant (LOLER)

From Fleet Compliance Desk · Great Britain · Last checked 22 October 2026

Equipment that lifts or lowers loads at work, including vehicle tail lifts and lorry-mounted cranes, must be thoroughly examined under the Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998 (LOLER).

How often

EquipmentThorough examination
Lifting equipment used to lift people, and its accessoriesAt least every 6 months
All lifting accessoriesAt least every 6 months
All other lifting equipmentAt least every 12 months
  • Alternatively, equipment can be examined under an examination scheme drawn up by a competent person.
  • An examination is also needed after exceptional circumstances that could affect safety, such as damage or failure, a long period out of use, or major changes.
Tail lifts

If anyone rides on the platform, for example an operator travelling with the load, the equipment is being used to lift people and the 6-month interval applies.

Good practice

  • Keep thorough examination dates in the same planner as safety inspections and MOTs.
  • For hired plant and equipment, ask for the current thorough examination report before it's used.
  • Thorough examinations are separate from routine maintenance and pre-use checks. You need all three.
