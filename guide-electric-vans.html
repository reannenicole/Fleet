Electric vans up to 4.25 tonnes

Batteries make electric vans heavier, so many go over the 3,500kg line that normally separates vans from trucks. Since 1 June 2026, the rules treat zero-emission vans up to 4,250kg much more like 3.5-tonne vans.

The current rules

  • MOT: zero-emission goods vehicles with a design gross weight of 3,501kg to 4,250kg take a Class 7 MOT instead of the annual test for heavy vehicles. For a new van, that normally means a first MOT at three years.
  • Tyres: these vans follow the 1.6mm tread depth rule for lighter vehicles, not the 1mm rule for heavy vehicles.
What counts as zero-emission

The regulations define it as a goods vehicle without an internal combustion engine, or with one that emits zero grams of CO2 per kilometre.

Check before you assume

For maintenance, the June 2026 changes affect testing and tyres in Great Britain. Check the current guidance before deciding any other rule, such as operator licensing, doesn't apply to these vans.
