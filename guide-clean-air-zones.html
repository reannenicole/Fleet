Clean air zones

Several English cities charge older, more polluting vehicles to drive in their clean air zones. Vans are charged in Class C and D zones, and lorries in Class B and above.

Standards that avoid a charge

  • Vans, minibuses and cars: Euro 6 for diesel, Euro 4 for petrol.
  • Heavy goods vehicles: Euro VI.

You can find a vehicle's European emissions standard using DVLA's vehicle information service (opens in a new tab).

Paying

  • Zones operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • Pay by 11:59pm on the sixth day after driving in, or up to 6 days before. Otherwise you may get a penalty charge notice.
  • Businesses with 2 or more vehicles can set up an account to check and pay for several vehicles at once.
London and Scotland

London's Low Emission Zone and ULEZ are run by Transport for London (lorries must meet Euro VI in the LEZ), and Scotland's four biggest cities run their own low emission zones. Check their own websites.
